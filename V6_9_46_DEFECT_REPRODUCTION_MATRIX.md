| REG-6945-01 | Three Why editor; Executive Web/PDF/DOCX/PPTX; Proposal Preview/PDF/DOCX | Saved scenario narrative -> Executive Value Story | Yes, live v6.9.45 artifacts | Narrative is persisted only through three legacy sibling fields, while the server has no canonical narrative projection that is written and read as one value.
