Competitive Research AI cannot approve findings, autonomously publish a Battlecard, change ROI, write Buyer Evidence, or expose competitive intelligence in customer outputs. Proposal AI cannot invent financial values, customer proof, customer quotes, customer commitments, or implementation commitments. Three Whys language remains opportunity narrative until supported by customer evidence; AI generation never makes it customer-confirmed.
