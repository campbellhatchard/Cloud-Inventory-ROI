# Discovery → Calculator Impact Map — ROI Model v2.8

This document, the in-app Impact Map, and its printable version are views of the same authoritative sources: `public/discovery.js`, `src/shared/questionnaire-roi-registry.js`, and `src/shared/roi-engine.js`. They do not define an independent economic model. The complete question-level matrix is in `QUESTIONNAIRE_ROI_MAP.md`.

Active methods: workforce productivity (labor-waste or throughput alternative), count labor, central write-off, central and field carrying, inventory turns, contribution-margin service value, service penalties/credits, expedite premium, downtime risk avoidance, order-error operations, first-time-fix, field leakage, field reconciliation, and IT displacement.

Economic classes are direct cost savings, recovered contribution margin, working-capital/carrying benefit, capacity value, and risk avoidance. Context and value-engineering questions have no direct ROI impact. The 99.5% accuracy target is an internal v2.8 calibration rule—not an industry benchmark.
